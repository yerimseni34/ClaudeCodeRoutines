## Weekly Insight

**Trend:** Next Fest (Oct 19–26) is pulling demos forward — Kill Breach, Dread of the Deep, Vaulted, Catacrypt and LaserWaves all dropped demos before it. The pre-Fest demo window is the new crowded slot. Also visible: subgenre blending (shooter + deckbuilder, action + strategy) rather than pure Vampire-Survivors clones, and consoles/1.0 exits for established survivors (DRG: Survivor, Genome Guardian 2).

**Gap:** Nothing this week combines *wave survival* with strong short-session (10–15 min) readability plus a distinctive non-fantasy/non-scifi theme *and* real tactical depth; most entries are either pure bullet heaven or heavy hybrids. A tight, readable top-down wave shooter with one strong hybrid mechanic remains underserved (inference from limited sample).

**Actionable insight for a solo dev:** If you're targeting Next Fest, your demo must be live and wishlist-capable *now* (≈2 weeks out), with a one-line hook that differentiates from bullet heaven (Vaulted's skill-card/stealth blend is the model). Copy LaserWaves' move: dated release + playable demo in the same announcement.
