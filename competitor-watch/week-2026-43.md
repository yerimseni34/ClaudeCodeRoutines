# Competitor Watch — Week 2026-43 (Oct 17–23, 2026)

Scope: wave survival shooters, roguelite action, top-down / isometric shooters.
Context: **Steam Next Fest (Oct 19–26)** is live, so this week's news is dominated by demo drops.

> Data caveat: wishlist counts are not public, and none of the sources surfaced them. Review counts appear only where a Steam page exposed one. Team sizes come from press coverage. "Momentum" is my inference from the signals listed, not measured data. Verify the details before acting on them.

## Games

### 1. Runz — sardin-ventures (2 brothers, Godot, self-published)
- **Status:** Demo live; releases Nov 13, 2026. Wishlists/reviews: not available.
- **This week:** Demo is in Next Fest. GamingOnLinux covered it ("every bullet in your magazine matters").
- **Momentum:** Rising. Press coverage plus a clear hook, with a release date within a month.
- **Study/steal:** The magazine *is* the build. Shop purchases fill ordered bullet slots, three identical bullets merge auto-battler style, and tags trigger synergies. 90 bullets, 23 synergies, 89 mods, 7 characters. It makes a twin-stick shooter's build legible in one glance.

### 2. Amazing Slot Survivor (A.S.S.) — TwoMenGames, published by LINE Games
- **Status:** Demo out Oct 16; Next Fest from Oct 20; release listed Nov 13 (Early Access also planned). Team size unclear. Wishlists/reviews: not available.
- **This week:** Demo update adds Stage 2, a bonus stage, a new spawner, a new character and a new skill, based on a month-long playtest that ended Sep 30. One early review called it a traditional bullet heaven that borrows from Megabonk, and noted screen tearing and no V-Sync option.
- **Momentum:** Steady and publisher-backed. The comparison to Megabonk suggests the novelty rests on the slot gimmick alone.
- **Study/steal:** A slot-spin as the weapon-acquisition loop. It turns the usual level-up menu into a gambling-flavored moment.

### 3. Pigeons Must Die! — Decaf Bagel Games (solo dev, Dylan)
- **Status:** Free demo live ahead of Next Fest; no release date. Wishlists/reviews: not available.
- **This week:** Demo opened just before Next Fest.
- **Momentum:** Rising on the strength of its pitch. The absent release date caps urgency.
- **Study/steal:** *Wave Contracts*, which let players wager currency on a benefit-plus-hazard pairing. Boss fights change the arena in phases. It's a solo-scope way to add risk/reward choices between waves.

### 4. Chained Beasts — Featherweight Games
- **Status:** Released Oct 15 on Steam, PS5, Xbox, Switch, and day-one Game Pass. A free Steam demo covers the Carthage arena. A French outlet reports a 35% launch discount until Oct 29, which I couldn't verify on Steam. Reviews: not retrieved.
- **This week:** Launch week, multi-platform. Covered by 80.lv (trailer transitions) and DLCompare.
- **Momentum:** Strong launch, helped by Game Pass distribution.
- **Study/steal:** The chain between co-op players is the mechanic. Coordination is the skill, and mistakes are funny. It's a single physical constraint that makes co-op worth watching on streams.

### 5. Horde Arena — FiveFiveSix Games
- **Status:** Early Access, Oct 7, $9.99, 11 reviews at 81% positive. Third-person co-op wave roguelite, up to 4 players. The developer discloses generative-AI use for music, localization and some assets.
- **This week:** Page shows no news posts. Reviews are early.
- **Momentum:** Quiet. 11 reviews after about 2 weeks is low traction.
- **Study/steal:** Class evolutions (two per class), a roulette between waves, and an account-wide currency for passive upgrades. Also a cautionary tale: a 3D wave shooter with no standout hook and no update cadence sinks quickly.

### 6. Engagement — (dev not confirmed)
- **Status:** Demo live; Nov 2026 release per Rogueliker. I could not confirm the developer name from the Steam page.
- **This week:** Included in Rogueliker's Oct 20 Next Fest roundup.
- **Momentum:** Unknown, with no traction data.
- **Study/steal:** A single capital ship as the player's body. Turrets attach to the hull, escorts can be recruited, promoted and lost, giving the survivors loop an emotional stake (six flagships, 25 modules, 15 evolutions).

### 7. Empty Shell 2 / Dreamfear / Appulse — adjacent top-down shooter roguelites (not new this week)
- Surfaced in search but dated months back (Empty Shell 2 announced 2025; Dreamfear demo Aug 2026; Appulse demo about 98 days old). Not counted as this week's news. Appulse's pinball-meets-shooter hook (Raw Fury) is worth a look.

## Weekly insight

**Trend:** Hooks are now attached to the *build or acquisition mechanic*, not the combat. Runz makes the magazine the build, A.S.S. makes the slot spin the build, Pigeons Must Die! adds wager contracts, and Engagement makes the ship the build. Moment-to-moment shooting is treated as solved. Nearly every title launches a demo into Next Fest, with Nov 12–13 clustering many releases (Runz, A.S.S., Delve Survivors, Topdeck Automat, Pools of Madness), which will crowd that week.

**Gap:** In this week's coverage, isometric entries are scarce. I found no isometric action roguelite shooter with notable momentum (the isometric hits were city builders or casual). The top-down twin-stick and bullet-heaven lanes are crowded, while isometric action with readable builds looks underserved. This is a thin-evidence read from search results, not a Steam-tag count, so check tag-page volumes before betting on it.

**Actionable insight for a solo dev:** Pick one build-defining mechanic that can be explained in a single GIF, and make it the whole pitch. Runz and Pigeons Must Die! both pair that with a small, well-scoped demo. Also avoid releasing within a few days of Nov 12–13. Aim for a demo during the next Next Fest and a release date at least two weeks clear of that cluster.
