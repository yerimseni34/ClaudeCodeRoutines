## WEEKLY INSIGHT

**Trend:** Next Fest week clustered many wave/horde roguelites into a Nov 5–13 launch window (Wielders of the Essence, Runz, Warhammer Survivors, Delve Survivors, A.S.S.), plus hybrids (survivors + base building, survivors + turn-based, extraction + roguelite). Hybridization is the dominant move; pure "bullet heaven" is crowded. Also notable: press coverage flagged "vibe-coded slop" in Next Fest, so quality signaling matters.

**Gap:** Few visible *isometric* action roguelites in this week's coverage — search found almost none confirmed (Fleshgrinder is an ARPG, not roguelite). Fixed-length, run-based wave shooters with a strong single hook are also underserved relative to open-ended survivors.

**Actionable for a solo dev:** Avoid launching Nov 5–13. Pick a window after the pile-up (late Nov/early Dec or Q1 2027), pick one physical/mechanical hook (like the chain) and a capped run length (like 20 waves) so the demo is a complete experience, and put the demo in the next Next Fest (Feb 2027).
