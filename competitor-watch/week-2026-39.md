## Weekly Insight

**Trend:** The differentiator battle in bullet-heaven/wave-survival has fully shifted from "more guns/more waves" to **narrative or mechanical hook + genre-blend framing**. Every rising game this week (Cloudbreaker, Spaceborn: Awakening, Appulse) wins press with one distinct, one-sentence hook layered onto the familiar loop — not through content volume. The stalling title (Crimson Moon) has budget and a well-worn genre-adjacent formula but no single hook, and it's the one struggling with reception.

**Gap that became visible this week:** Nobody in this batch is doing genre-blend + **horror** framing convincingly except Empty Shell 2, and it's pulling in press outlets (Bloody Disgusting) that never touch straight bullet-heavens. A horror-flavored wave-survival game is still an underserved lane — most "dark" entries (Vampire Survivors clones) are gothic/comedic, not actually tense.

**Actionable insight for a solo dev:** Don't pitch "bullet-heaven with X weapons." Pick one mechanical or narrative hook that fits in a single sentence (a grid-inventory twist, recoil-as-movement, story-IS-the-meta-progression) and build the trailer, store page, and press outreach entirely around that hook — then pitch it to outlets *outside* the roguelite beat (horror sites, sim sites, etc.) where the hook, not the genre, is the news.
