## WEEKLY INSIGHT

**Trend:** Next Fest (Oct 19–26) is shaping the week: demos are landing 5–7 days early (Pigeons Must Die!, runz), and the new entries lean on a *single-sentence mechanical twist* (draft your bullets; chained to a partner; bet currency on contracts) rather than raw content volume. Wave counts of ~20 per run are the norm for the format.

**Gap (inferred):** Direct top-down *wave-survival shooters with strong co-op* look thin — the co-op entries seen this week are mostly 3D/FPS (Horde Arena, Deadzone Rogue 2), while the top-down ones (runz, Pigeons, Genome Guardian 2) are single-player-leaning. Treat as a hypothesis; I didn't verify co-op status of each title.

**Actionable for a solo dev:** Ship your demo *before* the festival window and pair it with one memorable, clippable constraint. Pigeons Must Die! (solo) is the template: demo out ~5 days pre-Next Fest, press coverage already secured, and a premise that explains itself in a GIF. Also avoid Horde Arena's mistake of a diffuse tag identity — pick one lane.
