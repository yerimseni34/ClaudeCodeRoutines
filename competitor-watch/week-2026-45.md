# Competitor Watch — Week 2026-45 (Oct 31 – Nov 6, 2026)

**Scope:** wave survival shooters, roguelite action, top-down / isometric shooters.
**Data caveat:** Web search + page fetches only. Steam wishlist counts are not public, and most of these games are days old, so review counts are mostly "none yet". Team sizes are mostly not published; where I say "small/solo" it is inferred and marked. Anything marked *(unverified)* came from a single search snippet.

---

## Games this week

### 1. VOIDHOLD: Squad Survival Roguelite — Early Access, launched Nov 5
- **Dev / team:** NexusOne23 (self-published); team size not stated, looks solo/tiny *(inferred)*.
- **Status / numbers:** Early Access, $9.99 (launch discount 20% → $7.99 until Nov 15). **0 user reviews** at time of check. Free demo exists. EA planned until early 2027.
- **This week:** Launch + launch discount. Store page claims "content-complete and stable": 10 biomes, hidden 11th trial, 23 synergies from 7 mods, 31 achievements, 12 languages.
- **Momentum:** Unproven. No reviews yet, so launch visibility is the question. Steady, not rising.
- **Study/steal:** Squad of 4 soldiers + dog + drone as the "build" — party composition as the roguelite hook instead of a single character. Also: shipping EA already content-complete with a stated price-rise at 1.0.

### 2. Nova Survivors — Released Nov 5
- **Dev / team:** PandaRojas Studios; team size not stated *(likely tiny)*.
- **Status / numbers:** Released, $4.99 (15% intro → $4.24 until Nov 19). **1 review**, no score yet.
- **This week:** Launch day. 21 ships each with a unique weapon + ultimate, 100-level campaign over 4 worlds, endless mode with leaderboards, 32 languages. Discloses AI-generated music and AI-assisted translation.
- **Momentum:** Quiet. Bullet-heaven is a crowded lane and a $5 price point signals a volume play.
- **Study/steal:** Campaign *and* endless leaderboard mode in one package; 32-language localisation at near-zero cost. Note the AI-disclosure line on the store page — worth deciding your own policy up front.

### 3. Warhammer Survivors — Releases Nov 10 (demo live)
- **Dev / team:** Auroch Digital (Sumo Group) with poncle (Vampire Survivors) — **large/partnered**, not indie-scale.
- **Status / numbers:** Upcoming, $7.99, PC demo live. Platforms: PC, PS5, Xbox, Switch/Switch 2, iOS, Android, Game Pass day one. Wishlist count not public.
- **This week:** Release-date trailer, price, platform list and new characters (Kozlowski, Brother Luca, Gotrek, Sharynn Azurwrath) announced.
- **Momentum:** **Rising** into launch — licensed IP + poncle brand + Game Pass.
- **Study/steal:** The pricing: $7.99 is the new anchor for licensed survivors-likes. It also shows that a demo plus named-character reveals drives pre-launch interest. **Release-week risk for you:** it will dominate "survivors" tag visibility Nov 10–17.

### 4. RUNZ — top-down roguelite arena shooter, demo live; launch Nov 13 *(unverified)*
- **Dev / team:** sardin-ventures; team size not stated. Native Linux build.
- **Status / numbers:** Demo live. GamingOnLinux (Oct 12) gave no release date; a search listing showed **Nov 13** — confirm on Steam. No wishlist/review data.
- **This week:** Approaching launch after Oct Next Fest exposure.
- **Momentum:** Modest rise, press-driven (GamingOnLinux compares it to Brotato and Guncrypt).
- **Study/steal:** **The magazine is the build** — buy bullets, merge duplicates into stronger ones, enchant slots, order the firing sequence. A distinctive, readable mechanic that fits in one screenshot/GIF.

### 5. Brotato: Primal Dread (DLC) — Released Nov 2 (PC)
- **Dev / team:** Blobfish + Evil Empire (small team plus publisher).
- **Status / numbers:** $3.99 DLC. Base game is "Overwhelmingly Positive" with ~104k reviews across all languages (~29k English). DLC's own score not found.
- **This week:** DLC launch: new map with 20+ waves, new enemies, new dual-path "Evolution" stat (Mecha vs Mutation). Console/mobile in early 2027.
- **Momentum:** Steady — the genre's incumbent. One forum post hints at mixed DLC sentiment *(single anecdote, unverified)*.
- **Study/steal:** A cheap $3.99 DLC that adds a new *stat axis with a fork*, not just content — keeps a years-old game relevant and re-sells to the whole base.

### 6. CRYMELIGHT — dark Alice-in-Wonderland roguelite (FuRyu)
- **Dev / team:** FuRyu (established publisher).
- **Status / numbers:** Out on PS5/Switch 2. **Steam launch (planned Nov 5) missed** due to a delay in Valve's pre-launch review; PC date TBD.
- **This week:** Delay news.
- **Momentum:** Stalled on PC. Not clearly a shooter/top-down — included as a platform-risk signal.
- **Study/steal:** Practical: Valve review queues appear slow right now. Submit build + store page for review much earlier than you think.

### 7. Reverie in the Moonlight (room6) — Switch/Switch 2 port announced for Nov 19
- **Dev / team:** room6; team size not stated. Steam release Nov 2025.
- **Numbers:** Search snapshots show ~38–48 Steam reviews at ~96–97% positive (dated).
- **This week:** Console port date announced (Oct reporting, Nov 19 launch).
- **Momentum:** Slow-burn; a small, well-rated game getting a second sales wave via console.
- **Study/steal:** Roguelite + tower-defense hybrid, nightly waves framed as "defend your bed". A strong, memorable fantasy attached to a wave loop.

*Not covered:* Gravhounds (EA Nov 2) is third-person, not top-down. Horde Arena (Oct 7) is outside the 7-day window. Ignitement (isometric 2D horde survivor, Sørb) has a Steam release "later this year" with no date — worth a recheck next week.

## WEEKLY INSIGHT

**Trend:** The "survivors-like" lane is now a **price and platform race at $4–$8**: Nova Survivors $4.99, Warhammer Survivors $7.99, VOIDHOLD $9.99 with a launch discount, Brotato DLC $3.99. Meanwhile the games getting noticed (RUNZ, VOIDHOLD's squad, Brotato's Evolution fork) differentiate through **one structural twist on what "the build" is** — the magazine, the squad, a stat fork — rather than by adding more weapons. Also: two of this week's launches arrived with ~0–1 reviews, which suggests discoverability, not quality, is the bottleneck for new entrants.

**Gap:** Squad/party control is only just appearing in horde survival (VOIDHOLD, single-player only, no co-op planned). Top-down *co-op* squad survival with a strong hook remains thin in this week's data. Isometric specifically had almost nothing new this week. *(Based on a limited search; not a market-size claim.)*

**Actionable insight for a solo dev:** Pick one sentence-long twist on the build and make it GIF-able (RUNZ's magazine is the model), then **avoid launching Nov 10–17**, when Warhammer Survivors will dominate the tag. Ship a demo before launch and submit to Steam review early, given the CRYMELIGHT delay.
