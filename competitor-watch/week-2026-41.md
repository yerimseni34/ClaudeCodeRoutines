## Weekly Insight

**Trend:** October is crowded with Early Access → 1.0 exits and console ports (Genome Guardian 2, Sulfur, DRG: Survivor, Neon Abyss 2, Chained Beasts). Bullet-heaven/survivor is now a console-port genre, and genre hybrids (extraction + roguelite, immersive-sim + roguelite) are how newer entries differentiate.

**Gap:** Few cheap, tightly-scoped *isometric* wave-survival shooters appeared this week; most top-down/iso entries are arena-brawlers or hybrids. Horde Arena's weak launch (6 reviews) suggests plain "survive waves" without a distinctive hook doesn't cut through.

**Actionable for a solo dev:** Lead with a single-sentence mechanic hook that works as a 5-second GIF (echo clones, chained partners) rather than a genre label. And avoid launching into late October's crowded window; Steam Next Fest runs Oct 19–26 — a demo there is the cheapest wishlist lever.
