# Game Concept Brief — 2026-06-15

**Target dev:** Solo, Unity, 1–2 yrs experience, comfort zone = wave survival shooter.

## Research signal feeding these ideas

- Steam officially recognized "Bullet Heaven" as a genre tag (May 18, 2026) — the survivors-like has crystallized; saturated but legitimized.
- Mewgenics sold 1M+ in week 1, beat Hades II's concurrent peak. Cat content is hot.
- *House of the Dragon* S3 (June 21) and *Avatar: The Last Airbender* live-action S2 are dropping into the same window — dragons and elemental bending are in the cultural air.
- *Stranger Things* concluded its 9-year run — 80s-nostalgia hole opening up.
- FIFA World Cup kicked off June 11 — global soccer attention.
- TikTok niches trending now: "Cozy Gaming Desk Setups," "Clean Girl but Real Life," "Academic Weapon," Charli XCX "Rock Music" glitch format.
- Underserved sub-niches noted by indie market reports: low-stress horror, automation strategy, ultra-short-session loops, turn-based survivors-likes.
- Solo Unity benchmark: *Tangy TD* (March 2026) hit ~$245k gross / 28k units in week 1.

## TREND-BASED (3)

### 1. **Hatchling** — defend the clutch
- **Pitch:** You're a wyrm coiled around a glowing egg-pile while a kingdom's army keeps coming for it; the longer you survive, the bigger you grow.
- **Core mechanic:** Stationary-ish wave defense — you rotate/sweep a 360° fire-cone around your nest, breath weapon upgrades into chain-lightning / poison / wide cone, hatchlings emerge as autonomous minion units.
- **Why now?** *House of the Dragon* S3 drops June 21; dragon-mom power fantasy is peaking. Easier "monster-as-protagonist" angle than another knight game.
- **Solo feasibility:** **8/10** — single arena, radial aiming is familiar wave-shooter math, no procedural map.
- **Scope:** 3–4 months for a tight MVP, 6 for content.
- **Closest existing:** *Brotato* meets *They Are Billions* meets a tower-defense king-of-the-hill.
- **Fatal flaw:** "Defend the egg" is structurally a tower defense — risks feeling passive without the breath-weapon feeling genuinely powerful.

### 2. **Bender's Arena** — element-combo bullet heaven
- **Pitch:** Pick two elements, every weapon you pick up is a fusion of them, and the combo tree is the build.
- **Core mechanic:** Survivors-like, but instead of additive weapon slots you have an **element-pair commitment** (fire+earth = lava arcs, water+air = ice tornado, etc.). 6 elements → 15 unique combos = 15 "characters."
- **Why now?** Avatar TLA S2 returns to Netflix this month; *Avatar* mobile/console games are absent. Mainstream nostalgia, zero direct competition in the bullet-heaven space.
- **Solo feasibility:** **7/10** — element framework is reusable code, but 15 combos × meaningful differentiation is a content cliff.
- **Scope:** 6 months minimum for the combo matrix to feel justified.
- **Closest existing:** *Magicka* + *Vampire Survivors*.
- **Fatal flaw:** IP-adjacent without IP — risks looking like a knockoff and getting DMCA'd if visuals lean too close. Lean abstract/stylized.

### 3. **Hooligan FC** — terrace brawl survivor
- **Pitch:** It's the World Cup, you're a supporter, the rival fans are coming up the stairwell, and your only weapons are folding chairs and chants.
- **Core mechanic:** Wave survival in a stadium concourse; weapons are flares, scarves (whip), chairs (throwable), a megaphone that buffs nearby allies. "Chants" are area-effect buffs you activate by hitting rhythm prompts.
- **Why now?** World Cup 2026 (USA/Canada/Mexico) is mid-tournament June 15. Sports-violence comedy is a vacant lane; *Football Manager* dominates serious soccer, *FIFA* owns sim.
- **Solo feasibility:** **7/10** — one stadium tileset, generic crowd sprites, rhythm-buff is novel but bounded.
- **Scope:** 3 months for an MVP that goes viral on football TikTok, 6 to flesh out clubs/leagues.
- **Closest existing:** *Streets of Rogue* in a stadium.
- **Fatal flaw:** Hooliganism humor reads tasteless if real-world violence flares during the tournament — keep tone clearly cartoony, no real club logos.

## GAP-IN-MARKET (3)

### 4. **Conveyor** — automation × wave survival
- **Pitch:** Build the factory that builds the guns that kill the waves.
- **Core mechanic:** Between waves, lay belts/extractors/assemblers to manufacture ammo and turrets. During waves you grab guns off the production line and fire personally; bad logistics = empty mag.
- **Why now?** Automation games (*Factorio*, *Satisfactory*) have a huge audience but no good action-survival crossover. Indie market reports explicitly flagged "automation-focused" as underserved.
- **Solo feasibility:** **5/10** — belt logic is genuinely hard to get crisp; the genre's fans are unforgiving about pathing bugs.
- **Scope:** 1 year minimum, honestly. Don't underestimate.
- **Closest existing:** *Mindustry* (closest), with a *Risk of Rain* combat loop bolted on.
- **Fatal flaw:** Two genres at once doubles the polish bar; if either side feels shallow, both audiences bounce.

### 5. **Cozy Heaven** — low-stakes bullet heaven
- **Pitch:** A bullet heaven where you can't die, the "enemies" are crows in your garden, and the goal is collecting seeds for your sketchbook.
- **Core mechanic:** Auto-aim shooter loop, but timers are run-out-the-clock not survive-the-clock; "damage" makes flowers bloom, "boss" waves are storm clouds you disperse. Built around 8-minute sessions.
- **Why now?** "Cozy Gaming Desk Setups" is a top TikTok niche; *Stardew*-curious crowd has nothing in the action space. Low-stress horror trend signals appetite for de-fanged genres broadly.
- **Solo feasibility:** **9/10** — fewest mechanics on this list, art style does most of the work.
- **Scope:** 3 months is plausible. The risk is undershooting content depth.
- **Closest existing:** *Vampire Survivors* dressed as *A Short Hike*.
- **Fatal flaw:** Bullet-heaven fans want adrenaline; cozy fans don't shop in this aisle. The marketing has to invent its own shelf.

### 6. **Last Light** — turn-based survivor
- **Pitch:** Vampire Survivors as a chess problem. Every move you take, the swarm takes one too.
- **Core mechanic:** Grid-based, turn-based. You move 1 tile, all enemies advance toward you 1 tile, your auto-weapons resolve. Builds are about controlling timing and chokepoints, not DPS.
- **Why now?** *Vampire Crawlers* anchored "turn-based survivors-like" but it's a tiny sub-genre with one entrant. Open lane.
- **Solo feasibility:** **8/10** — grid + turn loop is much easier than realtime swarm physics. 500 enemies on a grid is cheap.
- **Scope:** 4–5 months for a strong MVP.
- **Closest existing:** *Vampire Crawlers*, *Into the Breach* (tonally).
- **Fatal flaw:** "Turn-based survivors" sounds boring in a 15-sec TikTok; trailer-ability is hard. Win that or die in obscurity.

## WILDCARD / EXPERIMENTAL (2)

### 7. **You Are The Swarm** — inverted wave shooter
- **Pitch:** You're not the survivor. You're the wave. Eat the hero.
- **Core mechanic:** Control a hive-mind that splits, regroups, evolves between waves; the "hero" is an AI auto-shooter mowing through you, and you win by overwhelming them before they level past you. Permadeath of the queen unit.
- **Why now?** Survivors-like fatigue is real and rising; the inversion is a clean Steam page hook. Plays with the same engine code the dev already knows.
- **Solo feasibility:** **7/10** — flock AI for the player-controlled horde is the hard part, but it's a single bounded system.
- **Scope:** 6 months.
- **Closest existing:** Genuinely nothing dominant. *Mewgenics* has horde-genetics energy but isn't inverted.
- **Fatal flaw:** Players struggle to feel "ownership" of a horde — risks feeling like an RTS without the strategy.

### 8. **Glitchwave** — rhythm-glitch survivor
- **Pitch:** A bullet heaven scored to a single track that stutters; every glitch is a damage spike.
- **Core mechanic:** Auto-shooter, but the track stutters at unpredictable beats and a "freeze frame" overlays the screen — during the freeze you draw a quick gesture (line/circle/zigzag) that triggers an attack. Run = one song.
- **Why now?** Charli XCX "Rock Music" glitch-format is the dominant TikTok video grammar right now; rhythm games are starved (post-*Crypt of the NecroDancer* lull). 3-minute runs = perfectly shareable.
- **Solo feasibility:** **6/10** — rhythm tech is finicky, audio latency on different hardware is a nightmare. Licensed music is a no-go solo; commission tracks.
- **Scope:** 6 months if audio middleware is wrangled early.
- **Closest existing:** *BPM: Bullets Per Minute*, *Pistol Whip*.
- **Fatal flaw:** Audio latency. If it doesn't feel tight on every machine, the game is broken.

---

## Recommendation in one paragraph

If shipping fast is the priority: **#5 Cozy Heaven** or **#3 Hooligan FC** — both are 3-month MVPs and trend-aligned right now (Hooligan with a hard tournament-window deadline). If betting on a 6-month differentiator: **#6 Last Light** is the lowest-risk standout because the sub-genre is real but uncrowded and grid math is solo-friendly. Avoid **#4 Conveyor** unless committing a full year — automation fans will smell a thin sim a mile away.
