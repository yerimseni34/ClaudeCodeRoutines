# Tool Radar — November 2026

**Window:** 9 Oct – 9 Nov 2026
**Audience:** solo Unity developer, Windows + RTX GPU
**Generated:** 2026-11-09 by routine "Tool Radar"

Scoring is **1–10 relevance for a solo Unity dev on Windows + RTX**. Items with no verifiable in-window release are skipped rather than padded.

**Quick take.** A quiet month for closed-source AI vendors (Meshy, Tripo, Rodin, Hunyuan3D, ElevenLabs, Suno, Cursor, Cascadeur all clustered their recent releases in May–Sep). The open-source **ComfyUI** ecosystem is where the real motion is, and **Blender** / **Blender-to-Unity** addons account for most of the in-window pipeline wins.

## Flagged upgrades

Direct answers to the three upgrade questions:

- **ComfyUI workflow:** **ComfyUI 0.44.0** (new native partner nodes + **Uni3C ControlNet for Wan**, which preserves geometry across views — the piece missing from most text-to-3D graphs). **NitroFusion2 for Z-Image-Turbo** is a drop-in speed/quality sampler node.
- **Blender → Unity pipeline:** **Blender 5.3** (Texture Layers + baking rewrite, targeted 10 or 17 Nov — edge of window); **Quadify** (retopologises AI/scan/CAD meshes to clean quads — the fix for "ComfyUI mesh has garbage topology"); **Tintline 2.2** (one-click auto-shade for low-poly colour atlases); **Game Asset Optimizer** (batch LOD + dual UV unwrap + decimation, Unity target).
- **Enemy AI systems:** nothing new in-window. Inworld / Convai / Charmed were quiet.
